# Gems of Utopia

The living portal of **Digital Utopia Serenity** — Soulcraft, High Garden Hacienda, the Sovereign Star Atlas, and the family soundtrack.

This is the interactive layer of the world. The archive and public record stay at [digitalutopiaserenity.xyz](https://digitalutopiaserenity.xyz) ([SerenitysOasis](https://github.com/Digital-Utopia-Serenity/SerenitysOasis)).

## Domains

| Layer | Domain | Role |
